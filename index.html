GCP for Data & AI Teams

Google Cloud is a tree of projects that each carry a bill and a set of permissions, with BigQuery charging by the bytes a query reads. Six leader sessions teach a data leader to read that sentence in every proposal and every bill, with no code and no account. Eight builder sessions do the work in the free BigQuery sandbox and on a laptop, on real Chicago taxi trips, and end in a bench that evaluates IAM requests the way Google documents and meters every byte a query reads.

For data and AI leaders, managers and architects who sign off on cloud plans and bills. Which project the work lives in, who may touch it, which meter is running, where the bill comes from, and the platform on one page.

What is real here 🧾

Pages about cloud bills and permissions should be exact about which of their own numbers are computed, which are priced, and which are modelled.

ClaimStatus
The dataReal. 18,362 trips from the City of Chicago's Taxi Trips (2013-2023), drawn with a fixed seed and shipped; BigQuery hosts the full table publicly. Used under the City of Chicago's data Terms of Use
Every byte countComputed from BigQuery's documented data type sizes on those rows and scaled to the table's 211,670,894 rows; a Python reference agrees with the browser to the byte
Every priceRead from Google's pricing pages (us-central1 default table) on 3 November 2026, with the date on every widget. Prices change: re-verify before relying on them
The IAM evaluatorWritten to Google's documented order, deny first then the union of allow policies, with Google's own permission lists for eight predefined roles; checked against 27 cases in the browser and in Python, and shown failing when deliberately broken
Builder outputsLaptop steps (pyarrow, DuckDB, Apache Beam, functions-framework) were really run, versions named. Sandbox outputs are marked "expected, your numbers will differ"
ModelledClustered block layout, the contents of the legacy basic roles, and slot reservations billed by the hour; each labelled where it appears
Tidewater GrocersConstructed: a fictional APAC grocery chain used for every hierarchy and IAM example
